New encounter form for your telehealth sessions

Hi Jennifer,

Starting today, you are part of the pilot of our new encounter form for telehealth sessions. The goal is to get your feedback before we roll it out to the rest of the team on Monday. Please tell me anything that feels confusing, slow or wrong.

Your Encounter Links in the app are already updated, so just open them the same way as always. Here is how the whole process looks:

1
Fill out one simple page
The youth, authorization and parent information come pre-filled, with Aliado's new design. You only enter the session date, start time and end time, and sign.
2
Confirm the parent's email and submit
You'll get an email when the document is sent to the family, and another one when the family signs it.
3
The family receives everything at once
The parent gets one email, in their language, with everything they need to sign. For a new family, that email includes the first-session paperwork (consent and Release of Information) together with the session signature. Current families only sign the session; they will not receive the initial paperwork again.
4
Pending encounters are now followed up by you
In My Billing Hours you'll see a new Pending Encounters tab with the sessions still waiting for a signature, including whether the family has opened the email. Each one has a Send reminder to family button you can use once per day. The administration will no longer follow up on these — each clinician now follows up directly with their families. Hours count only after the family signs.
✓ Our recommendation: send it while the family is still online
Before you end the session, open the encounter link, fill it out and submit it while the parent is still on the call. Ask them to check their email and sign right then. It usually arrives within a minute, and you'll get the confirmation that it's signed. It takes a couple of minutes and nothing is left pending.
✉ Please let your families know
Before, families received these documents from Jotform. Now they will come from info@aliadocommunityhealth.com. Please tell them to expect emails from that address, to check their junk or spam folder if they don't see it, and to save it as a contact.

If a family can't sign or something doesn't look right, please let me know.

Thank you for helping us test this!
